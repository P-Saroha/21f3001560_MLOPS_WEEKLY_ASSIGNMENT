WEEK 3 WORKFLOW
Integrating Feast Feature Store into the IRIS Pipeline
===============================

Problem Statement
-----------------

In Week 2 we used DVC to version datasets and model artifacts.

However, DVC only answers:

"Which dataset version was used during training?"

It does NOT solve another important production problem:

"How can we ensure the exact same feature engineering logic is used during both training and inference?"

If feature engineering is implemented separately for training and serving, it can lead to Training-Serving Skew.

To solve this problem, we integrated Feast Feature Store into the IRIS pipeline.
